GAMEZONE ARENA Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

GAMEZONE ARENA provides casual games, online Ludo, business and product listings, services, jobs, worker profiles, and related community features. This policy explains how information is processed when you use the app.

Information processed

  • Account data: Clerk provides authentication. The app uses an account identifier to associate games, listings, jobs, applications, reports, and saved items with your account.
  • Content you submit: names, descriptions, categories, contact choices, locations, service areas, work information, photos, reviews, reports, and resume documents.
  • Device permissions: camera, photo library, files, notifications, and precise location are accessed only when you use a related feature and after your device requests permission.
  • Advertising and diagnostics: Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers, advertising data, consent choices, and diagnostics to provide and measure ads.

How information is used

Information is used to operate games and marketplace features, save your content, display approved public information according to your visibility and consent choices, prevent fraud, moderate content, process reports, deliver notifications, measure ads, and support account operations.

Service providers and sharing

Information is processed by providers required to operate the app, including Clerk for authentication, Supabase for database and private-media storage, Google for ads and consent, and Expo for app delivery and notifications. GAMEZONE ARENA does not sell personal information.

Location and device choices

Location is used when you choose current location or a map position for a Business, Product/Service, Job, or worker profile. You may deny optional permissions. Features requiring a denied permission may not work until permission is granted.

Advertising choices

The app requests advertising consent where required, limits creative content to a G rating, and requests non-personalized inventory. When available in your region, you can reopen consent choices from Settings → Ad Privacy.

Public content and safety

Approved content may be public. Contact information is displayed only according to the relevant consent and visibility setting. The app provides block and report controls. Fraud, harassment, spam, and harmful or illegal content are prohibited.

Security and retention

Owner-scoped operations and row-level access controls protect private records. Private photos and documents use restricted storage and short-lived authorized links where applicable. Content is retained while your account or listing is active, subject to moderation, safety, and legal requirements.

Deletion

You can delete eligible listings from their owner screens. You can request account deletion from the Profile screen in the app. Account deletion removes associated app data and private media before the Clerk account is removed. If private-media cleanup fails, deletion stops so it does not silently leave an incomplete deletion; retry after connectivity is restored.

Children and target audience

The developer must configure the app's actual target audience consistently in Google Play and AdMob. The app does not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children. Parents or guardians may use the developer contact on the Google Play listing to raise a privacy or deletion request.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated when app behavior or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy, safety, or deletion questions, use the developer support contact shown on the GAMEZONE ARENA Google Play listing.
