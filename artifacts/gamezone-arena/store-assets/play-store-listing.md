# GAMEZONE ARENA — Google Play Listing

## App name

GAMEZONE ARENA

## Short description

Play games, create cartoon videos, and discover local work and services.

## Full description

GAMEZONE ARENA brings entertainment, creativity, and useful local connections
into one app.

PLAY GAMES

- Enjoy casual games designed for quick mobile play.
- Play Ludo and connect with other players online.
- Use sound, vibration, and simple touch controls.

CREATE CARTOON VIDEOS

- Choose a video from Camera, Gallery, or Files.
- Turn the complete video into a colorful comic-style cartoon.
- Keep the original movement and voice.
- Save the finished video or share it using supported phone apps.

DISCOVER LOCAL OPPORTUNITIES

- Browse businesses, products, and services.
- Find jobs and worker profiles.
- Create and manage your own eligible listings.
- Use location and maps only when you choose a location-based feature.

YOUR CHOICES MATTER

- Optional permissions are requested only when a related feature needs them.
- Control advertising privacy choices from Settings where available.
- Report or block inappropriate public content.
- Request account deletion from your profile.

Some features require an internet connection. Online availability, generated
video processing, advertising, and public listings may vary by region and
service availability.

## Recommended Play Console selections

- App or game: App
- Category: Entertainment
- Contains ads: Yes
- Target audience: 18 and over
- Contact details: Enter the developer's monitored support email in Play Console
