1. REAL ONLINE MULTIPLAYER

मौजूदा Ludo को वास्तविक online multiplayer बनाओ।

Players अलग-अलग Android devices/browser से एक ही match में connect होकर real-time खेल सकें।

Support:

- 2 Players
- 3 Players
- 4 Players

Example:

Device A → Player 1
Device B → Player 2
Device C → Player 3
Device D → Player 4

चारों एक ही Ludo game state में हों।

यह fake/local multiplayer नहीं होना चाहिए।

9. SYNCHRONIZED DICE
