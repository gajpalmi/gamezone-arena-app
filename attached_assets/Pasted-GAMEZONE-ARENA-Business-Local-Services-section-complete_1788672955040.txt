GAMEZONE ARENA में Business & Local Services section को complete करो। अभी केवल कुछ options दिख रहे हैं; मुझे सभी जरूरी Add, Customer, Contact, Sharing और Settings options चाहिए।

IMPORTANT:
पहले existing implementation को inspect करो। जो functionality पहले से बनी है उसे हटाना या redesign करना नहीं है। केवल missing functionality additive तरीके से जोड़ो।

1. MAIN BUSINESS SCREEN

स्पष्ट और बड़े buttons/cards बनाओ:

- ➕ Add Business
- 🛍️ Add Product
- 🛠️ Add Service
- 📋 My Listings
- ❤️ Saved
- 🔍 Search
- 📍 Location / City / Area
- 📞 Contact Business
- 📤 Share
- ⚙️ Settings

2. ADD BUSINESS

Business owner यह जानकारी जोड़ सके:

- Business name
- Owner/display name
- Category
- Sub-category
- Description
- Mobile/contact number
- WhatsApp number
- Address
- City
- Area/locality
- Opening hours
- Closing hours
- Business photos
- Logo
- Website/social link (optional)
- Services offered
- Price range
- Terms acceptance

Contact number को public दिखाने से पहले appropriate privacy/consent handling रखें।

3. ADD PRODUCT / SELL SOMETHING

Business owner/customer के लिए अलग “Add Product” option दें।

Fields:

- Product name
- Category
- Description
- Product photos
- Price
- Unit
- Available / Out of stock
- Location
- Contact number
- WhatsApp
- Optional delivery information

IMPORTANT:
यह real-money transaction/payment system अभी activate नहीं करना है। केवल product listing, enquiry/contact और sharing functionality रखें।

4. ADD SERVICE

Service provider अपना service listing बना सके:

- Service name
- Category
- Description
- Service area
- Price/rate (optional)
- Photos
- Contact number
- WhatsApp
- Availability/hours

Examples:
Plumber, Electrician, Carpenter, Mechanic, Tutor, Repair, Cleaning आदि।

11. SECURITY & PRIVACY

Existing Supabase RLS और Business security को preserve करो।

Cross-user data access नहीं होना चाहिए।

User A दूसरे user की private/pending listing edit/delete नहीं कर सके।

No Contacts permission.
No Call Log permission.
No SMS permission.
No unnecessary location permission.
No microphone permission unless an existing feature genuinely requires it.
